About

Hi, I’m Altrine! I’m an Information Systems graduate from Caraga State University with a passion for front-end web design, graphic creation, and keeping things highly organized.

While my degree gave me a solid understanding of how systems are developed, I don't pretend to be a hardcore backend coder. My heart is really in the front-end! I am familiar with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and my main focus is designing clean, user-friendly digital spaces. I'm looking for a role where I can bring my creative eye and my meticulous organizational skills to support a great team!
